# Insurance Management App: Database Fields

This document provides a comprehensive list of data fields required for an insurance management application, divided into **Insurance Provider Fields** and **Insurance Scheme Fields**.

\---

## 🏢 Insurance Provider Fields

These fields capture the corporate, regulatory, financial, and operational details of the insurance company.

### 1\. Basic Information

* **Provider ID**: Unique alphanumeric identifier (Primary Key).
* **Legal Name**: Official registered company name.
* **Brand Name**: Public-facing marketing or trade name.
* **Company Logo**: Image URL or file path for UI rendering.
* **Provider Type**: Category of organization (e.g., Public, Private, Mutual, Co-operative).
* **Description**: Brief overview of the provider's history or specialization.

### 2\. Contact \& Communication

* **Headquarters Address**: Full physical street address of the main corporate office.
* **Mailing Address**: Address for billing, official letters, or correspondence.
* **General Email**: Central corporate email address.
* **Support Email**: Dedicated customer service or grievance email.
* **Phone Number**: Primary corporate telephone line.
* **Toll-Free Number**: Customer support hotline for policyholders.
* **Website URL**: Official company website link.

### 3\. Regulatory \& Legal Compliance

* **License Number**: Government or authority-issued operating license identifier.
* **Tax ID / EIN**: Corporate tax identification or Employer Identification Number.
* **Regulatory Body**: Name of the supervising authority (e.g., NAIC, IRDAI, FCA).
* **Status**: Current operational standing (Active, Suspended, Inactive, Under Review).
* **Incorporation Date**: Official date the corporate entity was legally formed.

### 4\. Operations \& Partnership Management

* **Account Manager Name**: Assigned internal contact person managing the business relationship.
* **Account Manager Email**: Direct email address of the account manager.
* **Account Manager Phone**: Direct phone extension or mobile number of the account manager.
* **Contract Start Date**: Commencement date of the partnership with the provider.
* **Contract End Date**: Expiration or renewal date of the partnership agreement.
* **Commission Rate**: Agreed payout percentage or flat fee structure for the app platform.

### 5\. Financial Standing \& Ratings

* **Credit Rating**: Financial strength score from major agencies (e.g., AM Best, S\&P, Moody's).
* **Solvency Ratio**: Metric evaluating the provider's capability to meet long-term obligations.
* **Claim Settlement Ratio**: Historically recorded percentage of total claims approved and paid out.
