📋 Insurance Scheme Fields
These fields define the architectural details, financial constraints, and rules of specific insurance products (schemes) offered by a provider.
1. Scheme Overview
Scheme ID: Unique product identifier (Primary Key).
Provider ID: Foreign key linking the scheme back to its parent Insurance Provider.
Scheme Name: Commercial or marketing name of the specific insurance product.
Line of Business: High-level sector category (e.g., Health, Auto, Life, Property, Travel).
Policy Type: Specific sub-category type (e.g., Term Life, Comprehensive Auto, Indemnity Health).
Launch Date: Date the scheme officially becomes open for enrollment.
Expiry Date: Date the scheme closes to new applicants or transitions to grandfathered status.
2. Eligibility & Risk Rules
Minimum Age: Lowest acceptable age for a primary applicant at entry.
Maximum Age: Highest acceptable age for a primary applicant at entry.
Renewable Age Limit: Maximum age up to which an existing policyholder can renew coverage.
Target Audience: Intended consumer demographic (e.g., Senior Citizens, Corporate Employees, Families).
Geographic Restrictions: Specific states, provinces, countries, or regions where coverage is valid or restricted.
Medical Screening Required: Boolean flag indicating if a pre-policy medical checkup is mandatory (Yes/No).
3. Financials & Premium Metrics
Minimum Sum Assured: Lowest available coverage or death benefit amount.
Maximum Sum Assured: Upper cap on the coverage or death benefit amount.
Premium Frequency Options: Supported payment intervals array (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually).
Base Premium Rate Link: Reference ID to the actuarial pricing table or API calculator matrix.
Tax / GST Rate: Applicable statutory tax percentage applied to the premium.
Discounts Matrix: Available discount identifiers (e.g., No Claim Bonus, Multi-policy discount, Online purchase discount).
4. Coverage, Inclusions & Exclusions
Inclusions: Comprehensive structured list or markdown text of medical conditions, hazards, or assets covered.
Exclusions: Explicit list of conditions, events, or scenarios not covered by the policy.
Waiting Period Days: Number of days before specific coverages (e.g., pre-existing diseases, maternity) become active.
Co-Payment Percentage: The percentage of the claim amount the policyholder must pay out of pocket.
Deductible Amount: Fixed monetary amount the policyholder must pay before insurance coverage begins.
Available Riders / Add-ons: List of optional supplementary coverages that can be appended for an extra premium.
5. Policy Lifecycle & Terms
Minimum Policy Term: Shortest duration allowed for the contract (e.g., 1 year, 5 years).
Maximum Policy Term: Longest duration allowed for the contract (e.g., 30 years, Whole Life).
Grace Period Days: Number of days allowed for late premium payments before the policy lapses.
Free Look Period Days: Mandatory window during which the policyholder can cancel for a full premium refund.
6. Claims Management & Documentation
Claim Process Type: Settlement method supported (e.g., Cashless, Reimbursement, Hybrid).
TPA Details: Name and contact information of the Third-Party Administrator handling claims, if outsourced.
Required KYC/Onboarding Documents: List of documents needed for buying the policy (e.g., ID Proof, Income Proof, Vehicle RC).
Product Brochure URL: Direct link to the official product summary brochure or prospectus PDF.
